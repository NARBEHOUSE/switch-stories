## Switch adaptation

Text, artwork and presentation adapted for switch access by NARBE LLC. Created to expand access to stories for people who use switches and support a wider range of accessibility needs.

This edition adapts the names and wording for U.S. readers: Turuu (also printed once as Tuluu) becomes Benny; Munkhuush becomes Johnny; Uyanga becomes Emma; Bold becomes Max. The plot remains a story about friendship, generosity and inclusion. The illustration style and the characters’ Asian identities are retained. Artwork is adapted using AI, with wider scenes, matching costume initials, U.S. classroom clothing, cupcakes, an accessible doorway and simplified high-contrast images. The original costume-color dialogue is aligned with the illustrated costumes.

This is a still-picture edition without switch-triggered animations. With Read Aloud on, short groups of text continue on the same picture. With Read Aloud off, each group waits for a switch press. Physical pages always wait for a press. Image descriptions are supplied in both display modes.

## Original author’s note

The following is the author’s note from the supplied English edition; first-person statements refer to the original author:

This is the second book in a trilogy that was created under the“Angels with Wheelchairs”initiative. The trilogy includes stories about the everyday lives of people living with disabilities. After sustaining injuries in a car accident, I began to use a wheelchair and experienced many unexpected challenges in my daily life. Through this experience, I observed the prevalence of discriminatory attitudes in society toward people with disabilities and saw a need to change this problematic mindset. The goal of this book is to promote a more accepting and inclusive society by sharing the message that every individual, regardless of their physical ability, has a fundamental right to access education, learn, and prosper in society from an early age.
