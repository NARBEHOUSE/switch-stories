## Switch adaptation

Text, artwork and presentation adapted for switch access by NARBE LLC. Created to expand access to stories for people who use switches and support a wider range of accessibility needs.

This edition preserves the rhyming story, with “mum” changed to “mom” and minor punctuation and spacing corrections. Text is divided into short passages. The duplicate title illustration is consolidated into the cover; the original wordless carrot illustration becomes The End. The reading sequence contains 14 pages.

Artwork is adapted using AI from the supplied illustrations: wide scenes extend the backgrounds for different screen sizes, and simplified focal images support Simple High Contrast. The blue ant designs, family, books and story events are retained. Thin ant limbs are light-colored in Simple High Contrast so they remain visible on black without an outline. Both display modes have image descriptions. All pages are static, with no switch animations.

With Read Aloud on, successive story passages continue on the same picture. With Read Aloud off, each passage waits for a press. Physical page turns always wait for a press. Existing reading, scanning and sound settings apply.
