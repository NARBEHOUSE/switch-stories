## Switch Stories adaptation

Adapted for switch access by **NARBE LLC**, 2026. Created to expand access to stories for people who use switches and support a wider range of accessibility needs.

The narrative was shortened from approximately 919 to 409 words while retaining the journey, encounters, and resolution. The revised message establishes that Polaris already matters; her journey helps her understand the difference she makes. Text is presented in short groups without changing the reader’s switch controls.

Illustrations were redrawn and extended using AI image generation, using the supplied artwork as the visual reference. Changes include landscape compositions for different screens, consistent gray coloring and red eyes while Polaris is on Earth (printed pages 9–19), restored yellow coloring from page 20, a separate ending illustration, simplified high contrast pictures, and image descriptions. This adaptation has no switch animations. These changes were made by NARBE LLC, not by the original author, illustrator, publisher, or The Asia Foundation.
